School Plan

Academic Year 2026 - 2027

Vision for the Year

To provide quality education, strengthen academic excellence, promote discipline, and ensure the holistic development of every student.

Academic Plan

  • Conduct regular formative and summative assessments.
  • Improve SSC Board Examination performance.
  • Organize Olympiads and talent examinations.
  • Strengthen spoken English and communication skills.
  • Conduct monthly parent-teacher meetings.

Infrastructure Development Plan

  • Upgrade smart classrooms.
  • Enhance science laboratory facilities.
  • Improve computer lab infrastructure.
  • Expand library resources.
  • Develop sports and playground facilities.
